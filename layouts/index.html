I am a transportation safety researcher with a Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering. My work connects crash and injury analysis, vulnerable road-user safety, causal AI, and human behavior modeling.

I build reproducible research tools that combine crash, roadway, exposure, telematics, and spatial data. I use these systems to support clear and auditable decisions in transportation safety and evacuation planning.

Current projects

  • FMCSATelematics-informed crash risk for commercial vehicle safety
  • NCHRP 17-113 & 17-134Safe System practice, non-motorist analysis, and center-line buffers
  • BTSCRP BTS-39 & BTS-42Impaired-driving and automated enforcement guidance
  • AAA FoundationPre-crash conditions in pedestrian fatalities
